# Changelog

## v1.2 — July 11, 2026

**Unit 3 trigonometric identities** — Unit 3 (Trigonometric Functions) expanded
from 2 to 9 skills, closing the MCPS-syllabus gap on identities:

- Reciprocal and quotient identities
- Pythagorean identities
- Even/odd and cofunction identities
- Sum and difference formulas
- Double-angle formulas
- Half-angle formulas
- Verifying identities & solving trig equations

7 new problem generators. Numeric problems draw from Pythagorean triples so
answers are exact fractions; quadrant sign reasoning is required and wrong
signs surface named misconceptions; radical exact values use multiple-choice
with misconception-tagged distractors.

Also in this release: `macos-app/` folder added — macOS-specific packaging
(native launcher bundle + DMG build script), parallel to `windows-app/`.

Curriculum: **13 units, 50 skills** (was 43). Saved progress from earlier
versions loads unchanged.

Verified: TypeScript clean; 200 randomized runs per new generator; 500
math/sign spot-checks on the numeric generators.

## v1.1 — July 9, 2026

**Unit 12: Applications of Integration** — 5 new skills (area between curves,
volume by disks, net change, accumulation functions, average value).
Curriculum: 13 units, 43 skills.

## v1.0 — July 9, 2026

Initial release: 12 units, 38 skills. Single-file offline web app with
spaced-repetition scheduling, mastery gates, misconception-aware feedback,
and progress export/import. 8 bugs fixed during hardening (see spec §27).
